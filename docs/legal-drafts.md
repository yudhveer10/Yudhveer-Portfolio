## Terms and service delivery draft

- Descriptions and starting prices are invitations to discuss scope, not a binding project offer. A written proposal should define deliverables, fees, milestones, timeline, access, acceptance, revisions, support, and cancellation.
- Clients retain ownership of their own business, advertising, and platform accounts. The project agreement should specify ownership and licences for newly created work, third-party assets, and pre-existing tools.
- Advertising budgets are paid separately from service fees. Hosting, APIs, messaging, software subscriptions, and transaction fees may also be separate.
- Campaign leads, sales, rankings, savings, and other business outcomes are not guaranteed.
- Clients must provide lawful access, assets, content, and permissions. Customer messaging must follow consent and platform requirements.
- Availability for consultations is confirmed individually around existing employment commitments. A session is not booked until confirmed in writing.
- Digital products require a product-specific licence stating permitted use, sharing, modification, support, updates, and restrictions before sale. No product is currently listed for sale.

## Refund and cancellation draft
